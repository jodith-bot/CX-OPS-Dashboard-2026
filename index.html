How these numbers are calculated · data checks
  • Weeks run Sunday–Saturday. Week 1 is Jan 1–3. A week belongs to the month and quarter that hold most of its days (4 or more of its 7), so a week like Mar 29–Apr 4 counts in April and Q2. Every month is the exact sum of its weeks, and every quarter the sum of its months.
  • Trend colors: green means better, red means worse. Arrows show direction. TPH and CSAT going up are green (down is red); tickets, AHT, errors and replacements going down are green (up is red). Tickets handled per specialist going up are green.
  • Trend (pp) compares the selected period with the period right before it (week vs prior week, month vs prior month, quarter vs prior quarter). With no filter, cards show the latest complete week. Every change is shown in pp.
  • TPH = handled points ÷ (productive time + ticket time), the same formula as the TPH pivots. AHT = ticket time ÷ handled points. The pivot's "AHT(secs)" column reads hh:mm:ss as minutes, so it is recalculated here from Ticket time.
  • CSAT = good ÷ (good + bad) ratings. Averages across weeks are weighted by volume, never an average of percentages.
  • Errors are bucketed by shipping week. Errors by market only exist as a year-to-date total in the source, so that chart does not change with the period filter.
  • Replacements by market include missing and replacement meals. The missing vs replacement split only exists as a year-to-date total in the source.
  • Specialist filter applies to tickets, TPH, AHT and CSAT. Markets, contact reasons, errors and replacements have no specialist split in the source, so they stay team-wide.
